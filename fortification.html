What Is a FORTIFIED Roof?

FORTIFIED is a voluntary construction and re-roofing program developed by the Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety (IBHS). A FORTIFIED Roof is built to resist severe weather — including high winds, hail, and hurricanes — beyond minimum building code requirements.

In Mississippi, a FORTIFIED designation can qualify you for significant insurance discounts, especially on the Gulf Coast where hurricane risk is highest.

Durden Roof Care is trained and experienced in FORTIFIED roofing standards.

Three Levels of FORTIFIED

FORTIFIED Roof™

Strengthened roof deck, sealed roof deck, and upgraded shingles/metal to resist wind-driven rain and high winds up to 130 mph.

FORTIFIED Silver™

Includes Roof designation plus reinforced windows, doors, and gable ends to protect the entire building envelope.

FORTIFIED Gold™

Comprehensive protection including continuous load path connections from roof to foundation.
