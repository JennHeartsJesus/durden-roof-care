Our Process

How We Get the Job Done Right

From first call to final cleanup, here's what to expect when you work with Durden Roof Care.

1

Initial Contact

Call us, fill out the online form, or email. We'll respond quickly and schedule a time that works for you.

2

Complimentary Roof Inspection

Our team comes out, inspects your roof top to bottom, and explains exactly what we find — no pressure, no sales tricks.

3

Detailed Written Estimate

You receive a clear, itemized estimate covering scope, materials, timeline, and cost. No hidden fees.

4

Insurance Coordination

If you're filing a claim, we work directly with your insurance company and help you with the paperwork.

5

Contract Signing

Once you're ready, we walk you through the agreement, review every detail, and sign the contract together so the work can begin with confidence.

6

Professional Installation

Our experienced crew installs your roof with precision, following manufacturer specs and local building codes.

7

Final Walkthrough & Cleanup

We walk you through the finished work and make sure you're 100% satisfied.

8

Warranty & Follow-Up

Your new roof comes with manufacturer and workmanship warranties. We follow up to make sure everything holds strong.
