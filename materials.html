Roofing Materials We Install

Asphalt Shingles

3-tab and architectural shingles from brands like IKO, Atlas, GAF, Owens Corning, and CertainTeed. The most popular choice for Mississippi and Alabama homes.

Standing-Seam Metal

Premium metal panels with concealed fasteners. Extremely durable, energy-efficient, and rated for winds up to 140 mph. We use Gulf Coast Supply & Manufacturing (GCSM) or Metal Heroes.

Exposed-Fastener Metal

Cost-effective metal roofing (also known as Classic Rib) ideal for shops, barns, and commercial buildings. Available in dozens of colors. Sourced through Gulf Coast Supply & Manufacturing (GCSM) or Metal Heroes.

TPO Membrane

Single-ply thermoplastic membrane for flat and low-slope commercial roofs. Energy Star rated and highly reflective.

Modified Bitumen

Multi-layer flat roofing system with excellent waterproofing. Common on churches, strip malls, and multi-family buildings.

Synthetic Underlayment

High-performance underlayment beneath every roof we install for added protection against wind-driven rain.

Shingle & Metal Colors

Manufacturer Warranties

All materials we install come with full manufacturer warranties. Depending on the product, shingle warranties range from 25 years to lifetime coverage. Metal roofing typically carries 40–50 year paint and substrate warranties. We handle all manufacturer warranty registration and paperwork on your behalf — you don't have to choose or file anything.
