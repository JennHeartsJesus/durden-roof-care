Our Story

Family-Owned. Locally Trusted.

Durden Roof Care LLC was founded in Biloxi with a simple mission: give Mississippi homeowners and businesses a roofing contractor they can actually trust. No gimmicks. No high-pressure sales. Just honest work and durable results.

From the Gulf Coast to the Delta, our crew brings years of experience in residential, commercial, and industrial roofing. We show up when we say we will, we treat your property with respect, and we stand behind every job.

Whether it's a storm-damaged shingle replacement or a full commercial re-roof, we bring the same level of craftsmanship and care.
