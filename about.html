About Durden Roof Care

Rooted In Mississippi. Built On Trust.

Our Story

Quality Roofs, Honest Work

Durden Roof Care was founded on a simple idea: roofing should be done right the first time, by people who stand behind their work. From the Mississippi Gulf Coast to the Alabama line, we've built our name one rooftop at a time.

We believe in clear communication, fair pricing, and craftsmanship that lasts through every hurricane, summer storm, and Mississippi downpour.
