About Durden Roof Care

Rooted In Mississippi. Built On Trust.

A family-rooted, faith-driven roofing company serving homeowners, businesses, and churches across all of Mississippi.

Our Story

Quality Roofs, Honest Work, Lasting Relationships

Durden Roof Care was founded on a simple idea: roofing should be done right the first time, by people who stand behind their work. From the Mississippi Gulf Coast to the Delta, we've built our name one rooftop at a time — serving homeowners, businesses, and houses of worship with the same care we'd give our own family's roof.

We believe in clear communication, fair pricing, and craftsmanship that lasts through every hurricane, summer storm, and Mississippi downpour.
