Why Work With Us?

Durden Roof Care is more than a roofing company — we're a family. We value our crew members, invest in training, and believe in doing right by the people who make this company great.

  • Competitive Pay — Fair wages based on experience and skill.
  • Steady Work — Year-round projects across Mississippi.
  • Growth Opportunities — Move up from laborer to crew lead to foreman.
  • Respect & Safety — Safe jobsites and a team that has your back.
  • Faith-Driven Culture — We treat people the way we'd want to be treated.
